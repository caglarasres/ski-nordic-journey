Privacy policy

Effective 5 October 2026

In short. Ski: Nordic Journey has no accounts, no ads and no analytics of ours. Your progress stays on your device. Purchases are handled by Apple.

Who we are

Ski: Nordic Journey ("the game") is made by Caglar Kurt, an independent developer. For anything about this policy, write to kurtcaglar777@gmail.com.

What the game keeps on your device

Your settings, best score and distance, coins, the wardrobe pieces you own and what your skier is wearing are saved on your device only. We do not receive them, and deleting the game deletes them.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not show ads and the game contains no advertising software.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

In-app purchases

Coin packs and a few premium wardrobe pieces can be bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your card or payment details, your name or your Apple Account. Apple's handling of that information is described in Apple's privacy policy.

To talk to the App Store, the game uses Unity's in-app purchasing component. When a purchase is made or the Market is opened, that component may process limited technical information needed to carry it out, such as an app installation identifier, basic device information and your country or region. It is not used for advertising. See Unity's privacy policy.

When you contact us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to answer you and do not add you to any list.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. In-app purchases can be switched off on a child's device with Screen Time.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details of visits, such as IP addresses, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. The site itself sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
